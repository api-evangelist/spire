SPIFFE is supported by a broad ecosystem of projects, ranging from the core specifications and their reference implementation through to SDKs, deployment tooling, and the many open-source and commercial platforms that issue or consume SPIFFE identities. This page collects some of the more notable projects, grouped by what they do. Each one is marked as a SPIFFE official project, a community project, or a commercial offering.
