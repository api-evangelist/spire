Intro This blog post covers what SIG-Spec, the group that defines the SPIFFE standard, is working on right now and what we expect to focus on over the next 12 months. We encourage members of the community and industry to engage with the SPIFFE maintainers and provide input. This allows us to prioritize and focus on the features the community actually wants to see.
