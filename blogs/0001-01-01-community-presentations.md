The following presentations, podcasts, and blog posts demonstrate how people have used SPIFFE and SPIRE to address complex software infrastructure challenges. Introductions These presentations are designed to give some background for those who are not familiar with SPIFFE and SPIRE. SPIFFE and SPIRE: Architecture Deep Dive (VMware) Andrew and Evan offer a complete explanation of the SPIFFE and SPIRE architecture, including node and workload attestation, key management, and trust bundle rotation strategy.
