Status of this Memo This document specifies an identity and identity issuance standard for the internet community, and requests discussion and suggestions for improvements. Distribution of this document is unlimited. Abstract The SPIFFE standard provides a specification for a framework capable of bootstrapping and issuing identity to services across heterogeneous environments and organizational boundaries.
